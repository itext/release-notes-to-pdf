Release pdf2Data 2.1.10

Release date: January 14, 2021


In this release, we've extended the list of available selectors to add the possibility to extract text by its color. 

This version of pdf2Data also brings a number of parsing logic improvements that give a significantly better result in many use-cases. The two most important are advanced space character detection and further improvements to the field grouping that was released recently.
